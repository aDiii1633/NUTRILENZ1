How do I earn my first ₹10?+

Choose an available task, read its instructions, and complete each step. Submit the required screenshots. Once the task is verified and approved, the listed reward can be paid to your UPI ID.

Do I need to pay anything to join?+

There is no joining fee. Read each offer’s requirements before starting and only complete tasks you are comfortable with.

How will I receive my reward?+

Enter your preferred UPI ID in the reward flow after completing a task. Approved rewards are sent through UPI, so you can use the UPI app you already prefer.

How long does verification take?+

Submitted screenshots are reviewed before payment. Nutrilenz describes a typical wait of a few minutes to a few hours; timing can vary depending on the offer and review volume.

Can I complete more than one task?+

You can explore other available offers. Each task has its own eligibility and completion rules, and an offer may only be rewarded once. Check the instructions before starting.
